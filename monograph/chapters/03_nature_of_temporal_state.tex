\chapter{Nature of the Temporal State}
Let \((S,\prec)\) be an ordered relational domain and
\[
\Psi:S\to\mathcal H.
\]
Local transport is represented by
\[
\Psi_{n+1}=U_n\Psi_n.
\]
The state, transport operator, transition measure and bifurcation operator are treated as distinct mathematical types.
