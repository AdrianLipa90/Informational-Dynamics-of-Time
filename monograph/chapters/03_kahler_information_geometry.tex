\chapter{Kähler Information Geometry}
The temporal construction is opened on a state/information manifold rather than on an assumed spacetime. Let
\[
(\mathcal M,J,g_K,\omega)
\]
be Kähler, with
\[
J^2=-I,\qquad \omega(X,Y)=g_K(JX,Y),\qquad d\omega=0.
\]
At this stage, the coordinates of \(\mathcal M\) label informational or state degrees of freedom. They are introduced before the late Einstein closure and therefore remain distinct from spacetime coordinates.

For an oriented two-chain \(\Sigma\), the signed Kähler-area diagnostic
\[
\mathcal A_K[\Sigma]=\int_\Sigma\omega
\]
obeys
\[
\mathcal A_K[-\Sigma]=-\mathcal A_K[\Sigma].
\]
This gives the formalism an orientation-sensitive geometric object without yet promoting a preferred temporal handedness. Any temporal chirality claim is downstream of the dynamics.
