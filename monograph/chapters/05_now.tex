\chapter{NOW as Transition Localization}
Introduce the atomic transition measure
\[
\mathcal K_T=\sum_n q_n\delta_{s_n}.
\]
The active candidate defines
\[
\mathcal N=\operatorname{supp}_{\rm at}\mathcal K_T.
\]
The principal proof target is covariance of this support under the declared class of admissible monotone reparameterizations.
