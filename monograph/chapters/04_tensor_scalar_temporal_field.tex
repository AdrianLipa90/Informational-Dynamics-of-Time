\chapter{Tensor--Scalar Temporal Field}
The temporal configuration is represented by the typed pair
\[
\mathcal T=(\phi,\Theta_{AB}),
\]
where \(\phi\) is scalar and \(\Theta_{AB}\) is a tensorial state component on the Kähler information manifold. The indices \(A,B\) are state-manifold indices at this stage.

Evolution is parameterized by a pre-metric order parameter \(\lambda\):
\[
\lambda\mapsto\mathcal T(\lambda).
\]
The active minimal-dynamics candidate is
\[
\frac{d\mathcal T}{d\lambda}
=-\operatorname{grad}_{g_K}\mathcal I[\mathcal T]
+J\operatorname{grad}_{g_K}\mathcal H[\mathcal T].
\]
The two contributions are kept separate:
\[
\mathcal V_I=-\operatorname{grad}_{g_K}\mathcal I,
\qquad
\mathcal V_H=J\operatorname{grad}_{g_K}\mathcal H.
\]
The first is informational-gradient transport. The second is Kähler-rotated transport and is the earliest formal location where an orientation-sensitive dynamical effect can arise.

Two derivation debts remain explicit: the admissible informational functional \(\mathcal I[\phi,\Theta]\) and the phase/orientation functional \(\mathcal H[\phi,\Theta]\) must be constrained by upstream informational relations rather than selected to force a desired temporal topology.
