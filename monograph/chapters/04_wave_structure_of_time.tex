(D_L\Phi)_{ab}=\Phi_b-L_{ab}\Phi_a.

\section{Heterogeneous long-wave limit}

\[
\boxed{
M_{\rm eff}
=\left\langle M_e^{-1}\right\rangle^{-1}

\]

\[
\boxed{
\beta_{\rm eff}
=M_{\rm eff}^2

\qquad
