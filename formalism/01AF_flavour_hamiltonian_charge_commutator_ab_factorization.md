# 01AF — Flavour Hamiltonian, Charge Commutator and Aharonov–Bohm Factorization

Status: `EXACT_CHARGE_COMMUTATOR_THEOREM / EXACT_EQUAL_CHARGE_AB_FACTORIZATION / NEUTRINO_AB_NULL_EXACT / PHYSICAL_PMNS_HAMILTONIAN_BINDING_OPEN`

01AF follows 01AE. It promotes the static flavour gate

\[
U_f^\dagger Q U_f=Q
\]

to a continuous dynamical theorem and separates internal flavour oscillation from the electromagnetic Aharonov–Bohm factor.

## 1. Flavour evolution

Let

\[
a_\nu(\tau)=
\begin{pmatrix}
a_e(\tau)\\a_\mu(\tau)\\a_\tau(\tau)\end{pmatrix}
\in\mathbb C^3
\]

be the flavour-amplitude vector and let its local evolution be generated by an independently supplied Hermitian flavour Hamiltonian

\[
\boxed{
i\,\frac{d}{d\tau}a_\nu=H_f a_\nu,
\qquad
H_f=H_f^\dagger.
}
\]

Hence the finite evolution operator is unitary,

\[
U_f(\tau_2,\tau_1)^\dagger U_f(\tau_2,\tau_1)=I.
\]

01AF does not fix the numerical entries of `H_f`. A physical PMNS/mass-eigenvalue realization remains a downstream binding.

## 2. Charge expectation and its exact derivative

Let `Q` be a Hermitian electric-charge operator acting on the same flavour space and define

\[
\boxed{
\langle Q\rangle_\tau:=a_\nu(\tau)^\dagger Q a_\nu(\tau).
}
\]

Using

\[
\dot a_\nu=-iH_fa_\nu,
\qquad
\dot a_\nu^\dagger=i a_\nu^\dagger H_f,
\]

one obtains

\[
\frac{d}{d\tau}\langle Q\rangle
=i a_\nu^\dagger H_fQa_\nu
-i a_\nu^\dagger QH_fa_\nu.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\frac{d}{d\tau}\langle Q\rangle
=i\,a_\nu^\dagger[H_f,Q]a_\nu.
}
\]

Thus the exact charge-preservation condition is

\[
\boxed{[H_f,Q]=0.}
\]

On that surface,

\[
\boxed{\frac{d}{d\tau}\langle Q\rangle=0.}
\]

Exponentiating the evolution then reproduces the 01AE finite gate

\[
\boxed{U_f^\dagger Q U_f=Q.}
\]

## 3. Equal-charge flavour sector

If all three flavour components belong to one charge-degenerate sector,

\[
\boxed{Q=qI_3,}
\]

then for every Hermitian `H_f`,

\[
[H_f,Q]=q[H_f,I_3]=0.
\]

Consequently arbitrary unitary flavour redistribution inside the block preserves the electric charge expectation exactly.

This is the continuous version of the charge-degenerate mixing gate in 01AE.

## 4. Aharonov–Bohm connection as a central flavour factor

RF-M1 fixes the electromagnetic phase connection for charge `q` as

\[
\mathfrak a_{AB}=\frac{q}{\hbar}A.
\]

On the three-flavour equal-charge block this acts as

\[
\boxed{
\mathfrak A_{AB}
=\frac{q}{\hbar}A\,I_3.
}
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{[\mathfrak A_{AB},H_f]=0.}
\]

The AB transport factor along a path `C` is

\[
\boxed{
W_{AB}[C]
=\exp\!\left(i\frac{q}{\hbar}\int_C A\right)I_3.
}
\]

Because this factor is central in flavour space, the combined transport factorizes:

\[
\boxed{
U_{\rm total}[C]
=W_{AB}[C]\,U_f[C]
=U_f[C]\,W_{AB}[C].
}
\]

Thus flavour amplitudes may oscillate while the electromagnetic holonomy contributes one common phase to the whole charge-degenerate flavour multiplet.

## 5. Neutrino flavour block

For

\[
\boxed{Q_\nu=0_{3\times3},}
\]

one has identically

\[
[H_f,Q_\nu]=0
\]

for every Hermitian flavour Hamiltonian. The electromagnetic AB connection on the neutrino flavour block is therefore

\[
\boxed{\mathfrak A_{AB}^{(\nu)}=0,}
\]

and

\[
\boxed{W_{AB}^{(\nu)}[C]=I_3.}
\]

Hence a neutrino-flavour state may have nontrivial internal flavour-phase evolution

\[
U_f[C]\neq I_3
\]

while its ordinary electromagnetic AB factor remains the identity.

This extends the 01AE electromagnetic null-control from a static charge projection to the full continuous flavour evolution.

## 6. Block-diagonal generalization

For a general flavour space decomposed into electric-charge eigenspaces,

\[
\mathcal H_f=\bigoplus_q\mathcal H_q,
\qquad
Q=\bigoplus_q qI_{\mathcal H_q},
\]

the gate

\[
[H_f,Q]=0
\]

requires `H_f` to preserve the charge eigenspaces. Flavour oscillation may therefore occur inside a charge-degenerate block while cross-charge mixing is rejected by the electromagnetic source gate.

The corresponding evolution is block diagonal,

\[
U_f=\bigoplus_q U_q,
\]

and each block carries its own central AB factor

\[
W_{AB}^{(q)}[C]
=\exp\!\left(i\frac{q}{\hbar}\int_CA\right)I_{\mathcal H_q}.
\]

## 7. Relation to the 3 x (1,-1) AUX superposition

01AE supplies the eight bipolar AUX sectors

\[
|s_e,s_\mu,s_\tau\rangle,
\qquad
s_\alpha\in\{\pm1\},
\]

and the root-of-unity flavour projection

\[
Z_\nu=s_e+\omega s_\mu+\omega^2s_\tau.
\]

01AF acts on the continuous three-component flavour-amplitude layer associated with the same flavour labels. The discrete eight-sector register and the continuous `U(3)` flavour evolution are kept as separate representations. A physical lift between the full eight-dimensional AUX superposition coefficients and a measured PMNS evolution is a downstream construction.

## 8. Executable defects

A reference implementation should audit separately

\[
\Delta_H=\|H_f-H_f^\dagger\|,
\]

\[
\Delta_{HQ}=\|[H_f,Q]\|,
\]

\[
\Delta_{Qdot}
=\left|\frac{d}{d\tau}\langle Q\rangle\right|,
\]

\[
\Delta_U=\|U_f^\dagger U_f-I\|,
\]

and for equal-charge blocks

\[
\Delta_{ABcomm}=\|W_{AB}U_f-U_fW_{AB}\|.
\]

An adversarial Hamiltonian that mixes different charge eigenspaces must produce nonzero `Delta_HQ` and fail the charge-preservation gate.
