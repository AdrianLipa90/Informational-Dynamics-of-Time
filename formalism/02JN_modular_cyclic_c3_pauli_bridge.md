## 7. What remains open

This theorem does **not** prove any of the following:

- that periodic endpoint closure is the unique physical realization of every IDT (6pi) sector;
- that the temporal triad is literally physical three-space;
- that the temporal (C_3) carrier is literally the TIR flavour carrier;
- that the Pauli bridge supplies a metric/source coupling to Minkowski or Einstein dynamics;
- that the (F_3) character basis by itself fixes CKM/PMNS parameters;
- that a physical CP phase follows from the temporal carrier without an explicit sector-binding theorem.

Those remain separately typed source/physics gates.
