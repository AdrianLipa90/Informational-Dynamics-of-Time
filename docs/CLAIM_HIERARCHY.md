# Claim hierarchy

## C0 — Definitions

These are definitions introduced by the temporal formalism:

- `NOW := N` as the localized temporal realization event.
- `Psi_-` as the past-directed reconstructive temporal domain.
- `Psi_+` as the future-directed admissible temporal domain.
- `B_N` as the causal-wave bifurcation operator at NOW.
- `RECALL` as backward traversal/reconstruction over realized lineage.

Status: `DEFINITION`.

## C1 — Algebraic structure

Given unitary temporal transport,

\[
U_T^{-1}(\Delta\tau)=U_T^\dagger(\Delta\tau).
\]

Backward reconstruction is therefore algebraically available whenever the relevant state information is preserved.

Status: `ALGEBRAIC_CONSEQUENCE`.

## C2 — Temporal phase relation

For an energy eigencomponent,

\[
\Delta\phi_t=-E\Delta t/\hbar.
\]

Status: `STANDARD_PHYSICS_CLOSURE` when used as the conventional quantum phase-evolution relation.

## C3 — Wave/corpuscular temporal hypothesis

The framework proposes a model in which NOW is localized while past and future are represented as temporal wave domains joined through a realization/bifurcation boundary.

Status: `EXPERIMENTAL_HYPOTHESIS` until independently connected to physical measurement.

## C4 — Causal bifurcation hypothesis

The future domain is modeled as a set of admissible branches generated at a NOW boundary:

\[
\mathcal B_N:\Psi_{\mathrm{in}}\mapsto\{\Psi_{\mathrm{out}}^{(k)}\}.
\]

Status: `EXPERIMENTAL_HYPOTHESIS`.

## C5 — Memory/retrodiction relation

A realized lineage can be traversed backward computationally to reconstruct prior admitted states.

Status: `COMPUTATIONAL_DEMONSTRATION` when implemented and verified by lineage receipts; physical extrapolation requires a separate evidence class.

## C6 — Retrocausal hypothesis

The framework may suggest a future-boundary influence on earlier observable statistics, yet does not state such influence as an established result.

A physical retrocausal result requires a preregistered discrimination experiment that excludes ordinary causal communication, timestamp leakage, shared-clock artifacts, post-selection, and analysis leakage.

Status: `EXPERIMENTAL_HYPOTHESIS`.

## C7 — Einstein closure

The independently developed temporal branch is recombined with a spatial branch through

\[
x\oplus t\rightarrow(x,t)
\]

and compared against relativistic phase transport

\[
\phi=\frac{1}{\hbar}(\mathbf p\cdot\mathbf x-Et).
\]

Status: `STANDARD_PHYSICS_CLOSURE` for the comparison identity; any claimed derivation beyond that identity receives its own evidence status.
